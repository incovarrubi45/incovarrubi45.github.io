Artist Statement

Ideas, materials, experience, and the questions that follow.

My work explores the bigger picture: how we understand ourselves, navigate the world, and find meaning in life. My Christian faith informs my perspective, alongside my experiences and curiosity about the world around me.

Working across different mediums, I follow ideas, materials, and possibilities wherever they lead. Through my work, I invite people to think critically, question their assumptions, and consider why they see things the way they do. I want the work to be more than something to look at; I want it to be something to think about.

Approach

Each material and process brings a different set of possibilities. Sculpture, painting, drawing, and mixed media allow ideas to develop through making, testing, and reconsidering.
